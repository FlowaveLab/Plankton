## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a polished minimal logo for Plankton. Input image is biological subject reference ONLY, not an edit target: extract the rounded/tapered organism silhouette and its paired antenna-like projections, discard ALL microscopy texture and internal anatomical detail, replace its oblique pose with strict bilateral vertical mirror symmetry.
Create one strong iconic mark: a compact soft teardrop/oval central body, two elegant upward outward curved antennae of substantial thickness, and at most one pair of short simple lateral fins integrated into the outline. Give the central body ONE simple rounded white negative-space opening to make the silhouette memorable. Extremely restrained geometry, few smooth bold curves, strong filled silhouette, legible at 24px. Organic aquatic plankton identity; approachable, balanced, professional. Avoid looking like a devil, insect face, skull, crab or shield. No eyes or facial expression, no segments, no thin hairs, no intricate linework.
Flat two-color vector-like graphic, saturated warm crimson red approximately #DF2538 and pure white only. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no mockup objects, no decorative borders.
Deliver a clean wide presentation image with two equal adjacent square fields: left white field with large centered red logo; right crimson red field with the EXACT SAME mirrored-symmetric logo in white. Same mark, same scale, both centered with generous whitespace. No text, no lettering, no labels. This is a single logo shown in positive and reversed color treatments. Make both silhouettes absolutely symmetric left to right.
